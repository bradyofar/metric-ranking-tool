How this works

This tool scores your data fairly, even when different metrics use different units — like percentages and currency. It combines them into one clear ranking. Lower costs automatically score higher, so the final number always means the same thing: higher is better.

Each metric is standardised by comparing it against the group average. The distance from the average — measured in standard deviations — is the score.

  • Score above 0 — above average for that metric
  • Score below 0 — below average, room for improvement
  • Weights control how much each metric contributes to the total. Only the ratios matter — 1:2:3 gives the same result as 10:20:30.
  • Cost metrics are inverted — a lower cost becomes a higher score automatically
  • The final score is the sum of all weighted metric scores
