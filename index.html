How this tool works

TL;DR: We convert each metric into a Z-score (a standardized score). This lets you combine metrics that use different units—like percentages and currency—into one comparable index.

What's a Z-score?

A Z-score measures how far a value is from the average in terms of standard deviations.

  • Z = 0: exactly average
  • Z = +1: one standard deviation above average (better than ~84% of entries)
  • Z = -1: one standard deviation below average
  • Formula: Z = (value − mean) / standard deviation
About weights

Enter any positive numbers to express relative importance (e.g., 1, 2, 3). We automatically normalise them so they always add up to 100%, keeping the math stable.

Why costs are inverted

For cost metrics (CPM, CPC, etc.), lower values are better. We multiply their Z-scores by -1 so that "better = higher score" stays consistent across both performance and cost.
